Privacy Policy

Next Stop Stories for iPhone and iPad. Effective 25 October 2026.

Next Stop Stories does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDK. This page explains what the app keeps on your device and what it sends over the network.

Stored only on your device

  • Reading progress, saved stories, highlights, notes, your daily goal and streak.
  • Your settings (theme, text size, language) and the current ride.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location (optional)

Location is used only if you choose a stop for the stop alert. The app then asks for “While Using” permission. Your location is used on your device to show the arrival card and to schedule a system notification near the stop you chose. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Searching for a stop

When you search for a station, the search text is sent to Apple Maps to find matching stops. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

Ride and reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. No push notification service is used.

Network requests

The app downloads stories and cover images from the website where the stories are published. Like any web server, it may record the IP address and time of a request in standard server logs for security and operation. We do not combine this with any other data or use it to identify you.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
