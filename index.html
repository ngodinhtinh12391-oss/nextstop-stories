Next Stop Stories

Stories timed to your commute.

Next Stop Stories is a reading and listening app for people who read on the way to work. Tell it how long your ride is, or which stop you get off at, and it lines up stories that end before you arrive.

What it does

  • Ride Mode. Choose a trip length or arrival time. The app plans stories that finish before your stop and counts down while you read or listen.
  • Live Activity. The countdown stays on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Stop alert. Optionally choose your station. You get an alert about 500 m before it, even if the app is closed.
  • On arrival, narration pauses and your place is saved for the ride home.
  • Offline reading, read-aloud, adjustable text, highlights and notes. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Common questions

The stop alert didn't fire. Check that Location is set to “While Using” and Notifications are on for Next Stop in Settings. The alert triggers when you come within about 500 m of the stop you chose.

Do I need an account? No. Your progress, highlights and notes stay on your iPhone.

Does it work without signal? Yes. Stories you have opened are kept on your device, so tunnels don't interrupt you.
